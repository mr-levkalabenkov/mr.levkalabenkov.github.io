About

I am an SRE / DevOps Engineer with 7 years of experience supporting the reliability and operation of critical banking systems. I specialize in observability, incident management, Kubernetes operations, Linux, and production support in microservice environments.

I built monitoring from the ground up for a lending pipeline consisting of more than 30 production microservices processing over 1,000 applications per day. The monitoring platform reduced incident detection time to approximately three minutes while helping maintain 99.95% availability for critical services.

My strongest areas are Prometheus, Grafana, Kubernetes, Helm, PostgreSQL, OpenSearch, Linux, distributed incident analysis, and postmortems. I am currently developing further in IaC, GitOps, Terraform, ArgoCD, and CI/CD.

Current focus: platform engineering, Kubernetes operations, production observability, deployment automation, incident diagnostics, and reliable delivery of microservices.
